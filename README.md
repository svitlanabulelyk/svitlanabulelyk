## Svitlana Bulelyk

Physician, healthcare manager and digital health leader. Founder & CEO of
[Medics Help](https://medics.help) ([@medicshelp](https://github.com/medicshelp)), an AI platform that guides doctors and
nurses trained outside the EU back to practice in Europe.

### Experience
- **25+ years** of experience across clinical medicine, healthcare technology,
  regulatory consulting and international medical recruitment.
- **Randstad**: built the process for recruiting, recognising and onboarding
  international doctors and nurses for university hospitals in Germany and
  the Benelux countries. 400+ professionals placed.
- **EPAM Systems**: Vice President, Digital Health Transformation.
- **Medics Help (2026 to now)**: founder. Recognition planning, a voice AI
  coach for medical German, exam preparation and hospital introductions.
  Free for candidates, paid by employers. First cohort: early 2027.
